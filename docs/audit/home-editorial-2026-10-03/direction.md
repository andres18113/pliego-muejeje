# Home composition direction

Foundation is preserved: ultramar #283da8, yellow #f3df83, paper #f6f5f0, ink #252740, lavender #e4def4 and the existing secondary blue #bed9dd. Bricolage Grotesque remains display type; Roboto Flex remains reading and control type.

The supplied Headspace screenshots inform broad fields, purposeful asymmetry and alternating compositions. No layouts, colors, illustrations, characters, wording or assets are borrowed.

Left alignment for messages and metadata. Generous, deliberate space is paired with jackets that fill their scenes. Commerce controls retain their established contracts; no invented reviews, discounts or editorial facts. Rounded geometry belongs to large fields and the book's framing, rather than to every control.

Self-review: replaced the three-book hero collage, the conventional heading-over-card-row discovery and the interchangeable category cards. A plain text-and-product gallery would repeat the old hierarchy, so discovery instead uses a reading introduction beside a book-dominant shelf. Each subject uses a different arrangement; expressive symbols are reserved for philosophy and mathematics, where they have a reason.

Scope is Home only. Catalog, details, checkout, authentication, backend and contracts are protected. Shared product and commerce components are reused unchanged. Header changes are an explicit Home variant; the footer is a Home-specific composition.
